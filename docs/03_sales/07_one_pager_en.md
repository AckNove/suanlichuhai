# China Compute Globalization Services (CCGS)

**A specialist service provider connecting China's commercially available compute capacity with global AI computing demand**

---

## The problem

Cross-border compute procurement is rarely blocked by a shortage of GPUs. It is blocked by four gaps: requirements that are never specified properly, supply that cannot be verified, options that cannot be compared, and transactions that cannot be connected. Buyers cannot tell who actually controls the capacity. Sellers cannot tell who has a real budget. Both sides lack a credible layer of translation and coordination.

## What we do

**For buyers (overseas AI companies):** requirement analysis → compute specification definition → global sourcing → supplier verification → options comparison → risk identification → commercial coordination → ongoing procurement management.

**For sellers (Chinese compute suppliers):** overseas market research → customer development → qualified demand screening → opportunity introduction → commercial coordination → ongoing channel partnership.

What we deliver is a judgement you can compare, question and act on — not a price sheet.

## Three business lines

| Priority | Line | One line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | China compute supply going global | Helping Chinese intelligent-computing centres, GPU clouds and compute operators develop overseas and cross-border customers |
| 2 | Overseas data processed in China | Research on commercial and compliance pathways for inbound processing of overseas data and AI workloads (research only; no compliance assurance) |
| 3 | Global compute procurement and resource allocation | GPU procurement, sourcing, options comparison and ongoing procurement management for global clients |

## Target customers

- **Demand side:** overseas AI companies with real workloads in training, fine-tuning, inference, rendering or simulation.
- **Supply side:** Chinese intelligent-computing centres, GPU cloud providers and compute operators, plus Chinese-heritage compute operators in Singapore, Hong Kong and elsewhere.

## What sets us apart

1. **Buyer-side position:** no mark-up on the customer's price; we aim for the customer and the capacity owner to contract and settle directly.
2. **Verification before recommendation:** unverified capacity does not enter an options paper; where information is thin, we say so.
3. **Bilingual, two markets:** we understand both China's supply structure and overseas procurement practice.
4. **Responsible boundaries:** no lowest-price guarantees, no compliance conclusions, no involvement in circumventing export controls.

## What we are not

We do not operate data centres · do not buy GPU inventory · do not run a token factory or resell model APIs · do not hold client funds · do not help circumvent export controls · do not provide legal advice or claim that a transaction is "compliant".

## Current status and what you can rely on

- Corporate entity: **being established**; no invoicing or payment collection channel yet.
- Verified qualified supply: 0; verified qualified demand: 0; referral agreements signed: 0; completed transactions: 0.
- All current supply and demand leads are public information or internal research starting points. None has been verified first-hand and **must not be described as a partner or customer**.
- External messaging and commercial materials: **draft; not to be distributed until CEO approval**.
- This document is not an offer, a quotation or a supply commitment.

## How we work together

Introductory call (about 30 minutes) → written requirement or capacity summary → sourcing and verification → options comparison and risk notes → commercial coordination (if both sides decide to proceed) → longer-term cooperation agreed case by case in writing, on a project, success-fee or channel basis.

Fees are success-based. The specific basis is agreed case by case and confirmed in writing; no pricing is discussed in the first conversation.

# First-Contact Scripts (Chinese–English Pack · English Edition)

## Ground rules

1. **No fees in the first round.** Never mention commission, referral fees, points or revenue share. If asked, say: "The commercial model is agreed case by case in writing once there is a specific project."
2. **Under 150 words per message.** If it does not fit on two phone screens, cut it.
3. **One hook, one CTA.** The hook must be specific (drawn from the recipient's public information or a verifiable market fact). The CTA must be low-friction: reply with one line, a 30-minute call, or forward to the right colleague.
4. **Never mass-send.** Change at least one detail per recipient or do not send it.
5. All `[bracketed]` items are placeholders. Replace every one before sending.
6. This pack is a draft. Nothing may be sent externally until the CEO approves it.

## Scenario 1: Cold email to a supplier

**Use for:** Chinese intelligent-compute centers, GPU clouds, compute operators (including Singapore/Hong Kong entities).
**Goal:** a 30-minute capacity interview — not a price sheet.

### Version 1A — Direct to the capacity owner

**Subject:** A possible fit with [Company]'s [specific observation]

Hi [Name],

I'm [Sender] at CCGS, a compute globalization services firm. We help overseas AI companies find training and inference capacity, and we're looking at [specific observation] at [Company] to see whether it's within your sellable capacity.

We don't ask for exclusivity, and we don't discuss fees in the first round. I'd like 30 minutes to understand your GPU models, sellable volume, facility location and lead time. If there's a fit with overseas demand, we can then discuss how to work together.

Would a call next week work? If easier, just reply "happy to talk" and I'll send three time slots.

Best regards,
[Sender] | CCGS | [To be completed by CEO]

> Do not use: "large inventory", "plenty of capacity", "guaranteed orders", "lowest price in the market".

Hi [Name],

I'm [Sender] at CCGS. We connect Chinese compute capacity with screened overseas demand: we qualify a requirement to the point where it is worth a conversation before bringing it to a capacity provider, so you spend less time on inquiries that go nowhere.

Your [specific observation] suggests there may be an overlap. We don't ask for exclusivity, don't lock your other channels and charge nothing upfront. The first call is simply to understand your capacity structure and appetite for overseas work.

Would 30 minutes online work? Reply with a time that suits you.

## Banned words and phrases (check before sending)

| Banned | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| Guarantee / guaranteed / absolutely | Cannot be delivered | "We aim to", "within what can be verified" |
| Lowest price / cheapest / best price | You don't control the whole market; invites disputes | "Comparable options", "different pricing mechanics" |
| Authorized distributor / exclusive agent / official partner | Fabrication unless documented; legal risk | "Capacity provider", "counterparty", "pending verification" |
| Industry leader / world-class / top-tier | Unverified claim | Delete, or replace with a specific fact |
| Large inventory / plenty of capacity / ready to deploy now | Unverified capacity claim | "Sellable volume to be confirmed in writing by the supplier" |
| Fully compliant / compliant guaranteed | We do not give legal advice | "Compliance path to be confirmed by qualified counsel" |
| Government-backed / state-owned pedigree / strong connections | Unverifiable and high-risk | Delete |
| Inside channel / special relationship | Implies circumvention | "Public channels", "compliant pathway" |
| Commission / kickback / X% cut | First-round fee discussion; hard-coded rates are prohibited | "Success-based, agreed case by case in writing" |
| [Big tech] uses us / clients include [big name] | No authorization; unverifiable | "The types of clients we have served" (only with real cases) |
| Risk-free / can't lose / completely safe | Misleading | "Principal risks and mitigations" |
| Free (unqualified) | Implies everything is free | "A free preliminary sourcing note after the first call" |
| 100% / all / every | Absolutes | Specific scope plus conditions |

# Non-Exclusive Channel Cooperation & Opportunity Protection — One Pager

## 1. Who we are

A business service team focused on China compute going global (legal entity being set up, founded by [CEO Name]). We research overseas and cross-border compute demand, turn public leads into verifiable requirements, and connect them with providers that can deliver. **We do not own data centres, do not hold GPU inventory, do not resell compute, and do not custody funds.**

## 2. Cooperation model (non-exclusive)

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| Exclusivity | **Non-exclusive.** You may work with any third party; we do not ask for exclusive agency and do not restrict your other channels |
| Our role | Overseas demand research and screening, demand-side qualification, introductions, meeting and material coordination, deal coordination up to pre-contract stage |
| Your role | Provide accurate, verifiable resource information; respond to introduced requirements within 5 business days where possible; contract and settle directly with the demand side where conditions allow |
| Funds | We **never custody** customer or supplier funds; we do not collect or remit payments |
| Pricing | We **do not mark up**; your pricing goes directly to the demand side |

## 3. Opportunity registration and protection

1. **Registration**: for a specific project, we register in writing (email is sufficient), stating the demand party (name/sector), requirement, expected scale and timing.
2. **Confirmation**: you confirm in writing within **5 business days**. Confirmation is effective on receipt; absence of objection within the window is deemed confirmed.
3. **Protection period**: **12 months** from confirmation. During this period we coordinate the registered opportunity; you do not approach that demand party directly or via a third party.
4. **Scope**: only the registered opportunity; it does not cover your other customers or channels.
5. **Termination**: protection ends on expiry, your written waiver, or the demand party's termination.

## 4. Fees (not part of this round)

- No fee discussion and no fee in round one;
- For a concrete project, both sides sign case-by-case written documents specifying fee model, calculation basis, payment milestones and refund conditions;
- If both the supply and demand sides pay us for the same project, we disclose this **in writing in advance** and obtain both parties' consent;
- No oral statement constitutes a fee basis.

## 5. Confidentiality and data

- Both sides keep non-public information confidential (pricing, customers, capacity, contract terms);
- We disclose your name and resource profile to a demand party only with your written consent;
- Cross-border data handling follows applicable law and your compliance requirements; we make no compliance guarantee and do not facilitate export-control circumvention.

## 6. Boundaries and exit

- This document is not a contract, agency authorisation or exclusivity commitment; formal cooperation requires a signed written agreement;
- Either side may terminate the cooperation intention with 10 business days' written notice;
- Termination does not affect protection of already registered opportunities or performance of signed agreements.

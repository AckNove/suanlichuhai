# 30-Minute Resource Call Agenda (Dengjia AI)

## Time allocation

| Time | Segment | Purpose | Talking points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 min | Opening and identity | Build trust, state boundaries | "We do business matchmaking for China compute going global; entity being set up. Today is resource fact-finding only — no fees, no pitch." |
| 3–7 min | Our value in one sentence | Show we bring screened demand | "We do not promise customer volume. Every requirement passes four filters before it reaches you: technical fit, budget credibility, timing, decision path." |
| 7–22 min | Nine-question verification (core) | Obtain verifiable resource facts | Go through the 9-question sheet; ask for the basis before the number; mark "not public" when declined |
| 22–26 min | Demand-side snapshot (no names) | Create interest without over-sharing | "We are qualifying a GPU expansion requirement from a national ICT/data-centre operator in Southeast Asia; models and volumes are still being confirmed. We do not disclose the name before written registration." |
| 26–30 min | Next steps and boundaries | Agree follow-up and written record | "I will send the call notes within 24 hours for your confirmation. If both sides want to continue, we first sign a non-exclusive summary, then discuss specific projects." |

## Question order (easy to sensitive)

1. Entity and authorisation (who can sign)
2. GPU models and specs
3. Sellable volume
4. Deployment locations
5. Pricing basis
6. Delivery lead time
7. Network and cluster
8. Overseas capability
9. Non-exclusivity and resale rights (last, most sensitive)

## Handling three common pushbacks

| Pushback | Response |
|---|---|
| "Tell us who the customer is first" | "We do not disclose the demand party before a written opportunity registration is confirmed — that protects both sides. Once confirmed, you get enough information to judge." |
| "How many customers have you closed?" | "Zero closed deals, zero signed contracts — we do not exaggerate. Round one is resource verification; we prove the model with one deal before talking scale." |
| "Give us a commission number" | "No fee discussion in round one, so neither side is anchored to a number without basis. For a concrete project, we agree case by case in writing." |

## Must-do within 24 hours after the call

1. Send call notes (per question: their answer / evidence status / to be confirmed);
2. Record in the system: supplier_verification + evidence_registry (source = this call, trust = MEDIUM/self-claimed);
3. Update status: `RESEARCHED → NEEDS_VERIFICATION` (never straight to VERIFIED);
4. Log a project_activity (time, channel, participants, conclusion, next step).
